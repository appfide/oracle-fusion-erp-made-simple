# Getting help

- **Learning questions or a confusing lesson** — open an issue using the *Content error* template, or the
  *Feature request* template for a new topic. Link the lesson (copy the address from your browser).
- **Something broken on the site** — open a *Bug report* issue with your browser and device.
- **Security problem** — do **not** open an issue. Follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
- **Code of Conduct concern** — email code@appfide.com (see [CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md)).

This is a free community project maintained by Appfide Private Limited. We answer on a best-effort basis and aim
to triage new issues within a week. For help with your own company's Oracle Fusion system, please contact your
Oracle administrator or Oracle Support — we cannot see or support individual Oracle environments.
